Your music. No ads.

Spotless is a free music app for Mac and iPhone. Millions of songs, podcasts and audiobooks, with no ads. This edition signs in with publik and sends usage counts to publik; the no-account edition is fjenn/spotless.

Everything you listen to, in one app

No ads, ever

No audio ads, no video ads, no banners. Spotless plays the audio itself instead of embedding a web player.

Your library stays yours

Sign in with publik once, then play. Your library, playlists and history live on your device. Sign in to YouTube only if you want your existing library.

Mixes made for you

Personal mixes, a weekly discovery list, radio from any song and smart shuffle, all worked out on your device from what you play.

Lyrics

Synced lyrics that follow the song, with romanization for Japanese, Korean and Chinese.

Podcasts and audiobooks

Follow shows, jump by chapter, change speed, skip sponsor segments. Free audiobooks from LibriVox and the Internet Archive.

Offline

Download songs, albums, playlists and episodes and listen without a connection.

Sound your way

Equalizer with presets, speed and pitch, reverb, crossfade and a sleep timer.

Bring your playlists

Import playlists from a link or from the data export of another streaming service.

Made for the Mac

Mini player, menu bar controls, media keys and global shortcuts. Updates install themselves.

Questions

Why is it free?

Spotless is open-source software under the GPL, built on Limusic and other open projects. It has no servers to pay for: the app runs on your device and fetches music straight from the source, so there is nothing to charge for and nothing to sell.

Where does the music come from?

From YouTube Music's catalogue. Spotless talks to it directly and plays the audio itself, which is why there are no ads. Podcasts come from their publishers' feeds, audiobooks from LibriVox and the Internet Archive.

What happens to my data?

This edition signs in with publik and sends usage counts (app opened, active session) to publik. Apart from that there is no analytics and no crash reporting, and we run no server. To work, the app asks third-party services directly, for example YouTube for music and lyrics services for lyrics, and they see that request. Details are on the privacy page.

Do I need a YouTube or Google account?

No. Signing in is optional and only brings in your YouTube Music likes and playlists. Your sign-in is stored in the system Keychain and only sent to YouTube.

Is it safe to install?

The Mac app is signed and notarized by Apple, and every line of its source is public. Updates are signed too: the app refuses an update whose signature does not match.

Is Spotless connected to Spotify or YouTube?

No. Spotless is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Spotify, YouTube or Google.

Something is broken. Where do I get help?

See the support page. In short: open an issue on GitHub.

Spotless is free software under the GPL-3.0. A fork of Limusic.
